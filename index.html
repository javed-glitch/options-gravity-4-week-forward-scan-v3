Tap a symbol to jump to its card. One dot per weekly expiry (next 4 Fridays). ● Good = options positioning backs the price holding up · ● Mixed = no clear edge · ● Weak = options lean down, no nearby support.
Plain-English guide. Buy at = the nearest price levels below today's price where lots of traders have bets (a "floor"). Sell at = the nearest levels above (a "ceiling"). Chance it fills = chance price drops to the buy level that week. Chance it holds = chance it then stays above it. Chance it gets there = chance price rises to the sell level. All measured from today's price, so a full buy-then-sell is less likely than either step. ▲▼ = level moved vs the week before. ⚠ earnings week = results can gap the price, so odds are less reliable.
Are options pricey? Traders are paying for much bigger moves than the stock has really been making, so big swings are expected (often earnings). Enough buyers and sellers? THIN = few people trade at that price, so use a patient limit order. Can I trust this level? SOLID, OK or WEAK, from the two rows above. Claude's call is a judgement on buying physical stock: the net return must beat the 0.30% FX cost (0.15% each way).
